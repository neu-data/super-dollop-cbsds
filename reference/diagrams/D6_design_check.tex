\node[m=\cw, below=5mm of pa] (pa1) {\textbf{Covered in this course}\\
Continuous: paired t-test, Wilcoxon signed-rank (\dref{D2})\\
Binary: McNemar's test (\dref{D3})\\
Analyse the within-pair differences.};
\node[warn=\cw, below=5mm of re] (re1) {\textbf{Do not} run a separate test at each time point, or treat the measurements as independent people.};
\node[further=\cw, below=4mm of re1] (re2) {Simple summary measures per person (change, slope, average) analysed with basic methods; or mixed-effects models or GEE.};
\node[warn=\cw, below=5mm of cl] (cl1) {Ignoring clustering makes standard errors too small, so CIs are too narrow and p-values too small.};
\node[further=\cw, below=4mm of cl1] (cl2) {Mixed-effects (multilevel) models, GEE, or cluster-robust standard errors. Analyse cluster summaries if there are few clusters.};
\node[warn=\cw, below=5mm of te] (te1) {Do not drop censored people or analyse ``event yes/no'' when follow-up time differs.};
\node[further=\cw, below=4mm of te1] (te2) {Survival analysis: Kaplan-Meier curves, log-rank test, Cox proportional hazards regression (hazard ratio).};
