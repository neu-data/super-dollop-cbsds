}

\node[warn=25.4cm, anchor=north west] at (0,-11.7) {\textbf{Use these diagrams to structure your thinking, not as automatic rules.}
Look at the data with plots, think about how they were collected, and report an effect estimate (difference, ratio, slope) with its 95\% confidence interval, not only a p-value. Sample size alone does not decide between a t-test and a rank-based test, and neither does the p-value of a normality test.};
